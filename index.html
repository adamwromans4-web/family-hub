Extra Notes for Nicky
😤 4pm Witching Hour

What is Restraint Collapse?

Austin holds it together all day at nursery. When he gets home he releases all that tension — this is restraint collapse. It's not bad behaviour, it's exhaustion coming out safely. Best response: calm, empathetic, low demand.

📅 4pm Schedule
4:00 — Low-key welcome
4:10 — Snack immediately
4:30 — Free play / TV OK
5:00 — Tea time
5:30 — Quiet time
6:30 — Wind-down
7:00 — Bath
🍎 Snack Ideas
• Banana & peanut butter
• Cheese & crackers
• Breadsticks & hummus
• Apple slices
• Oatcakes
• Raisins & rice cakes
• Milk & a biscuit
🎮 Low-demand Activities
• Playdough
• Sticker books
• Duplo
• Sensory bin
• Water play
• Drawing
• CBeebies
⚠️ Avoid at 4pm
• Errands / shopping
• Lots of instructions
• Noisy environments
• Overstimulating play
• Saying no repeatedly
• Screen transitions
• Rushing
Reminders
✅ Stay calm — your calm is contagious
✅ Acknowledge feelings: "I can see you're really tired"
✅ Don't escalate — match with lower energy
✅ Offer a snack before anything else
✅ Low demands, simple choices only
✅ Physical comfort if he wants it
✅ This passes — usually within 20 minutes
