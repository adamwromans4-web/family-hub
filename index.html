Austin's 4pm plan 🕒
The witching hour — why it happens and what to do
Why does it happen?
Austin has been holding it together all day. By 4pm his "good behaviour fuel" is empty — Restraint Collapse. Add low blood sugar and you have a meltdown. The fix: connection, food, calm activity.
The 4pm schedule
4:00
Reconnect — 10 mins 1-on-1
Floor play or cuddles. Fills his connection tank. No phones.
4:15
The Appetiser Snack 🧀
High-protein snack to stabilise blood sugar before dinner.
4:30
Low-dopamine play 🚿
Keep him in the kitchen. Sink station, stickers, Tupperware sorting.
5:00
Dinner on table by 5pm sharp 🍽️
Don't let it slip past 5pm. The snack buys time but not unlimited time.
Quick snack ideas — protein & fat
🧀 Cheese sticks & cucumber
🍌 Banana with peanut butter
🥚 Hard-boiled egg
🍓 Greek yogurt & berries
🍎 Apple with almond butter
🧀 Cheese cubes & crackers
Distraction activities
🚿 Sink station
Let him wash plastic containers — keeps him busy 20+ mins.
🌟 Sticker station
Tape paper to a cupboard and let him go wild with stickers.
🧁 Muffin tin sort
Muffin tray and dry pasta or pom-poms to sort.
🔑 Golden rule
If Austin is acting out at 4pm, he's out of good behaviour fuel — not being naughty. Connection and food first, always.
