Austin's 4pm plan 🕒
The witching hour — why it happens and what to do
Why does it happen?
Austin has been holding it together all day — at nursery, being good, following rules. By 4pm his "good behaviour fuel" is empty. This is Restraint Collapse, not naughtiness. Add low blood sugar from not eating since lunch and you have a meltdown waiting to happen. The fix is simple: connection, food, calm activity.
The 4pm schedule
4:00
Reconnect — 10 minutes 1-on-1
Floor play or cuddles. This fills his "connection tank" so he doesn't have to act out to get attention. No phones, just him.
4:15
The Appetiser Snack 🧀
High-protein snack to stabilise blood sugar before dinner. Bridges the gap to 5pm. See snack ideas below.
4:30
Low-dopamine play in the kitchen 🚿
Keep him close while dinner is finished. Sink station, stickers, or sorting Tupperware. Calm, no screens, low demand.
5:00
Dinner — on the table by 5pm sharp 🍽️
Don't let it slip past 5pm. The snack buys you time but not unlimited time. Aim for him eating by 5.
Quick snack ideas — protein & fat
🧀 Cheese sticks & cucumber
🍌 Banana with peanut butter
🥚 Hard-boiled egg
🍓 Greek yogurt with berries
🍎 Apple with almond butter
🧀 Cheese cubes & crackers
Easy distraction activities
🚿 Sink station
Let him "wash" plastic containers in the sink. Keeps him busy and calm for 20+ minutes.
🌟 Sticker station
Tape a piece of paper to the cupboard door and let him go wild with stickers.
🧁 Muffin tin sort
Give him a muffin tray and some dry pasta or pom-poms to sort. Simple, calm, keeps hands busy.
🔑 The golden rule
Keep things calm and low-demand at 4pm. If Austin is acting out, it's almost certainly because he's out of "good behaviour fuel" for the day — not because he's being naughty. Connection and food first, always.

😴 Sleeping much more
🍽️ Suddenly very hungry
😭 Clingy or unsettled

Olivia's growth tracker 📏
Height, weight and pre-teen growth spurt signs
🌸 Signs of a pre-teen growth spurt (age 11–14)
😴 Sleeping much longer
🍽️ Eating significantly more
😤 Mood swings / irritable
🦵 Aching legs or joints
😩 Tired even after sleep
👗 Clothes suddenly too small
🧠 Difficulty concentrating
🤸 Clumsiness — new height
At almost 13, Olivia is likely in or approaching her main adolescent growth spurt. Mood changes and fatigue at this age are very often growth related — not just "being a teenager". Extra food, iron-rich meals and patience go a long way.
💡 What helps during a spurt
🥩 Iron-rich foods — red meat, spinach, lentils, fortified cereal
🥛 Calcium — milk, cheese, yogurt for bone development
😴 More sleep — don't fight the lie-ins, her body needs it
🚶 Light movement — stretching helps growing pains
💬 Check in gently — mood swings can feel overwhelming at this age
